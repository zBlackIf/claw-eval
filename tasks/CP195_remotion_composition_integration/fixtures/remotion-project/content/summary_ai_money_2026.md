# 5 Ways to Make Money in 2026 (with AI)

**Title:** 5 Ways to Make Money in 2026 (with AI)
**Channel:** Sabrina Ramonov
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xl57Pki6ChE

## Key Takeaways

### 1. AI-Powered Content Creation
Create and monetize content using AI tools for scripting, editing, and distribution. Low barrier to entry with potential for significant passive income through YouTube, blogs, and newsletters.

### 2. AI Automation Agency
Build an agency that helps businesses automate repetitive tasks using AI. Services include chatbot development, workflow automation, and data processing pipelines. Average project value: $5K-$50K.

### 3. AI SaaS Products
Develop niche software products powered by AI. Focus on specific pain points in industries like real estate, healthcare, or legal. Monthly recurring revenue model.

### 4. AI Consulting & Training
Offer expertise to companies transitioning to AI workflows. Includes strategy consulting, team training, and implementation guidance. High hourly rates ($200-$500/hr).

### 5. AI-Enhanced Freelancing
Use AI tools to 10x productivity in traditional freelance services (copywriting, design, development). Deliver more value in less time, commanding premium rates.

## Summary

The video emphasizes that 2026 is the year where AI transitions from novelty to necessity. Each method leverages AI as a force multiplier rather than a replacement. The key insight is to combine domain expertise with AI capabilities to create unique value propositions that pure AI cannot replicate.
