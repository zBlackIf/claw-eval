# Partner Commission Rules

## Tier Structure

| Revenue Tier (Monthly) | Commission Rate | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| $0 - $10,000 | 15% | - |
| $10,001 - $50,000 | 12% | - |
| $50,001 - $200,000 | 10% | - |
| $200,001+ | 8% | $25,000/month |

## Calculation Rules

1. Commission is calculated per-partner, per-vendor, per-month
2. Revenue tiers are based on total monthly revenue across all services
3. Commission cap applies to the total commission amount per partner
4. New partners get +2% bonus for first 6 months

## Approval Workflow

- Commissions < $5,000: auto-approved
- Commissions $5,000 - $15,000: manager approval
- Commissions > $15,000: VP approval via Lark workflow

## Payout Schedule

- Calculation: 5th business day of following month
- Approval deadline: 10th business day
- Payout: 15th business day
