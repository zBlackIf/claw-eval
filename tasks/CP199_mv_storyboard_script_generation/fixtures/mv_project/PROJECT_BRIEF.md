# MV Project Brief: "I WILL LISTEN TO MY BABY"

## Song Information
- **Title**: I WILL LISTEN TO MY BABY
- **Genre**: Pop / Family Ballad
- **Mood**: Warm, innocent, joyful, touching
- **Duration**: ~3 minutes (180 seconds)
- **Theme**: Father-daughter bond, featuring father-daughter duet
- **Key emotional notes**: Unconditional love, growth, innocence, deep familial connection

## Lyrics Summary (Key Sections)

### Verse 1 (Father)
Every morning when you wake / I see the world inside your eyes
Your little hand holds onto mine / together we will reach the skies

### Chorus (Duet)
I will listen to my baby / every word you say
I will listen to my baby / each and every day

### Verse 2 (Daughter)
Daddy when I'm feeling scared / you always make it go away
Your voice is like a lullaby / that turns the night into the day

### Bridge (Father)
Years will pass and you will grow / but this I promise you, my dear
No matter where the road may lead / my heart will always find you here

### Final Chorus (Duet - with harmony)
I will listen to my baby / every word you say
I will listen to my baby / now and always

## Visual Style Requirements
- Color palette: Warm tones (golden hour, soft pastels)
- Animation style: 2D illustrated, watercolor-like textures
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Target total video duration: 60 seconds (condensed MV)

## Deliverable Requirements
The storyboard document (STORYBOARD.md) must include:
1. At least 8 scenes/shots
2. Each scene must specify: scene number, visual description, corresponding lyrics/audio, duration in seconds, camera movement/transition
3. Scenes must follow the song structure (verse1 -> chorus -> verse2 -> bridge -> final chorus)
4. Total duration of all scenes must sum to approximately 60 seconds (+/- 5 seconds)
5. Include a METADATA section at the top with project info
6. Include a TECHNICAL NOTES section at the bottom

## Reference: Narrative Arc
The MV should tell the story of a father and daughter's journey together:
- Opening: Father watching over sleeping baby
- Early childhood: First steps, learning together
- Middle childhood: Playing together, father teaching daughter to sing
- Growing up: School days, father supporting from behind
- Present day: Grown daughter and father singing together
- Closing: Full circle - father's love endures
